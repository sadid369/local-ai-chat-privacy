Privacy Policy — Local AI Chat

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Local AI Chat ("the app") is a client for self-hosted AI servers such as Ollama and LM Studio. You choose the server that receives your requests. This policy explains how the app handles conversations, local settings, and advertising data.

The advertising sections apply to Android versions that include Google AdMob. Earlier versions without AdMob do not perform the advertising-related data processing described here.

Conversations and settings

  • No app account or sign-up is required.
  • Chat history, saved server addresses and ports, selected models, and app preferences are stored in the app's local storage on your device.
  • When you send a message, the app sends that message and conversation context to your configured AI server to generate a reply. It also contacts that server to test the connection and retrieve available models.
  • The server operator controls how that server processes and retains requests. If you connect to a server operated by someone else, their data practices apply to the information you send.
  • The app does not send chat messages, conversation history, or saved server connection details to AdMob or a developer-operated backend.
  • The expiry time of a rewarded ad-free period is stored on your device.

Advertising and Google's services

Ad-supported Android versions use Google AdMob for banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. This can include:

  • Your device's IP address, which can indicate approximate location.
  • Advertising ID, app set ID, and other applicable device or account identifiers.
  • App and ad interactions, such as launches, taps, and video views.
  • Diagnostics about app and SDK performance.

Advertising data is separate from your AI conversations. The SDK encrypts its data in transit using TLS. Processing depends on applicable consent choices, SDK settings, and Google's policies.

For details, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information.

Consent and your choices

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request updated consent information and display privacy messages when required. Where available, use Settings → Ad privacy choices to revisit your choices. Android also provides controls to reset or delete your advertising ID.

Watching a rewarded ad is optional. Earning the reward hides banners and interstitial ads for 30 minutes. This does not erase previously processed data or disable all advertising-SDK or consent-service activity. Chatting does not require watching a rewarded ad, and local-server chat can work without an internet connection if your server remains reachable.

Network access and security

The app connects to your configured AI server and, in ad-supported Android versions, to advertising and consent services. The current AI-server connection uses unencrypted HTTP. Use it only on a trusted network; other parties with access to that traffic may be able to read it. Advertising traffic uses separate connections and does not make AI-server traffic encrypted.

The Android app and its advertising SDK use network access, network-state, and advertising-related permissions, including advertising ID. The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or precise-location access.

Data retention and deletion

  • Local chat history remains until you delete it or clear the app's data. You can delete conversations from the chat-history panel and saved servers from Settings.
  • Use Android's app-storage settings to clear Local AI Chat's local data, including preferences and the saved reward expiry. Uninstalling normally removes app-local data. Device backups or transfers may retain or restore app data depending on your Android settings; manage those copies through your device or backup provider.
  • Deleting local history does not delete copies or logs held by your AI server. Manage those on the server or contact its operator.
  • Google controls retention and deletion of the advertising data it processes under its privacy policy. Clearing this app's data does not automatically delete information already processed by Google. See Google's deletion and retention information.
